# EQ-04: Path-dependent exits (close-based stop, latched trailing stop, time exit) and a causal market-trend gate
- Status: planned | Priority: P1 (infrastructure used by EQ-01, EQ-05) | Effort: M | Source(s): library `audits/02_PURUJIT_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` (P3, P4, P6, P9), `strategies/06_PURUJIT_AFTER_HOURS.md`, `strategies/01_EA1904.md` (5% stop, 10/20 holds), `audits/01_EA1904_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` (E4, E5); https://github.com/PurujitAggarwal/PEAD_strategy, https://github.com/EA1904/pead-sp500-research

## 1. Thesis & what we take from the source (and what we deliberately do NOT take)
Thesis: the framework only has a fixed-session exit (`with_fixed_hold`, `pead/engines/equity.py`) or per-cell decay windows. A stop/trailing/time-exit layer lets us test whether truncating losers or locking gains improves risk-adjusted return of a PEAD book, and gives the EA1904 and Purujit ideas a correct implementation.
Take: (a) percent stop from the entry price; (b) trailing stop with persistent ("latched") activation - the corrected form of Purujit's trailing rule; (c) max-holding time exit; (d) an ATR-style volatility stop as an option; (e) a market trend gate (SPY above 100-day SMA) implemented causally; (f) exit-fill honesty (stops trigger on closes, fill at that close, never at the trigger price).
Do NOT take: Purujit's hold of 4,500 elapsed minutes (calendar minutes, crosses weekends); ex-post volatility scaling over the future window (P7); the reciprocal short ROI `(entry/exit)-1` (P9); the `.any()` filter bug (P4); sequential trade compounding (P8); EA1904's engine that never charges exits (E5). Shorts are out of scope here (borrow unmodelled).

## 2. Evidence grade & known defects in the source
Purujit grade B/C, no pinned CAGR; EA1904 grade C/D with the stop only in a withheld model. No evidence in the library that stops add value; the audit supplies the defects and counterexample tests: only-risk-off events produce no trades; BMO 08:00 uses the prior day's SPY close, not the same day's; price path 100 -> 103.5 -> 101 must trigger a +3% activated / 2% trailing stop; two same-day +2% trades at 50% NAV each give +2% not +4.04%. Repo's own evidence points against tight exits for this signal: holds of 40 and 90 sessions scored 8.7% / 7.9% vs 10.6% for 60 (STEADY_DRIFT section 9), i.e. the drift is slow, and the doc records that trend-gate/cash choices changed results through exposure, not skill.

## 3. Exact rules
Let entry be the day0 close (row `e`), `v_k = prod_{j=1..k}(1 + r_{e+j})` the raw-return wealth path of the position (raw `ret`, including dividends, from `uni.ret_raw`; delisting returns as recorded in CRSP `ret` on the last row), `k = 1..H`.
`ExitRule(max_hold=60, max_hold_cal_days=None, hold_col=None, stop_pct=None, trail_activation=None, trail_amount=None, atr_mult=None, atr_window=14)`. `max_hold_cal_days` (used by EQ-05) exits on the last trading row on or before `day0_date + N` calendar days; `hold_col` names an event column carrying a per-event hold (calendar days) so a long/short book can have different holds per leg.
- Time exit: `exit_k = min(H, last row of the stock)`, identical to `with_fixed_hold` when only `max_hold` is set (must reproduce it exactly; test).
- Percent stop: first `k` with `v_k <= 1 - stop_pct`; exit row `e + k` (fill at that close; no gap-through credit).
- Latched trailing: `peak_k = max(1, max_{s<=k} v_s)`; `active_k = (peak_k >= 1 + trail_activation)` (latched: once true stays true, fixing P6 where activation was recomputed on the current bar); exit at first `k` with `active_k and v_k <= peak_k * (1 - trail_amount)`. Library defaults to test: activation 3%, trail 2% (Purujit config) but note those are the source's, not tuned here.
- ATR stop (option): `atr_proxy = mean(|ret|)` over the `atr_window` sessions ending day0 (CRSP has `bidlo/askhi` but they are bid/ask-based lows/highs and not used); stop at `v_k <= 1 - atr_mult * atr_proxy`. Default off.
- Priority: earliest of the triggers; ties resolved stop before time. Exits are determined only from `v_1..v_k` (no information after `k`).
- After a stop-out the capital is idle (cash) and the same stock is not re-entered inside the same event (events are independent, the next event for the stock is a new position as already allowed by the engine).
- Trend gate (`trend_gate_mask`): index level `I_t = cumprod(1 + vwretd)` on `uni.calendar`; `gate(day0) = I_{day0-1} > SMA_100(I)_{day0-1}`; applied to every event whenever the gate is defined (NaN, i.e. first 100 sessions -> no trade); no `.any()` shortcut. Uses the previous completed close even though entry is at the day0 close (strictly causal, cheap).
Costs: unchanged engine costs at the (earlier) exit; a stop does not get a cheaper cost. Optional `stop_extra_bps` (default 0, stress 25) for stop slippage.

## 4. Data requirements & availability in THIS repo
Available: daily raw returns for every panel row (`EquityUniverse.ret_raw`), `panel_permno` (needed to cut at a stock's last row, verified in `EquityUniverse`), `entry_row_idx`, `exit_row_idx`, `vwretd` and `calendar`. Missing: intraday highs/lows (stops cannot trigger intrabar; accepted and documented), true opens for stop fills (available only through REF-dsf-reader), delisting returns beyond what CRSP `ret` carries (`dsedelist.parquet` exists in `data/raw_wrds/crsp/`, not integrated; the repo's fixed-hold keeps delistings in P&L via the last row).

## 5b. Build vs. change vs. restructure
(1) NEW: `ExitRule`, `with_path_exits`, `exit_reason`; `pead/strategies/gates.py`; `steady_drift_exits.py`; `tests/test_path_exits.py`; `trend_ok` and `atr_proxy` columns in EQ-02's table.
(2) CHANGE: **engine cap causality (important)**: `pead/engines/equity.py::run_equity_backtest` feeds `exit_cal` into `cap_open_book` (per-day headroom of a quarter's new positions and the `carry` of earlier ones). With a fixed hold the exit day is known at entry; with a stop it depends on FUTURE prices, so admission headroom would use information not available at entry (a look-ahead in the leverage cap). Fix: events carry both `exit_row_idx`/`exit_date` (actual, used for P&L, costs, exposure) and `sched_exit_row_idx`/`sched_exit_date` (the time-exit date, used ONLY by the cap and `carry` computations); the cap is conservative (a stopped-out position keeps its budget reserved until the scheduled date; cash is simply idle). Regression risk: none when the two coincide (all existing strategies); pin with the NAV tests. Also `pead/strategies/base.py::EquityStrategy.backtest` and `params()` (additive; existing strategies have `exit_rule=None` and produce identical output - pin with `tests/test_framework.py` NAV tests); `pead/reporting/attribution.py` line ~39 uses `hold_ret` (per-event precomputed holding return from the decay-window build) which is STALE for any fixed-hold or path-exit strategy; verify and switch the excess-return attribution to the engine's own position P&L (`positions` has `gross_pnl`, `ret_on_notional`), otherwise reports for stopped books will be wrong.
(3) RESTRUCTURE: REF-exit-rule-hook (shared with EQ-01/EQ-05); move the permno-run-end helper (`last`/`run_end`) out of `with_fixed_hold` into a shared `_run_end_rows(uni)` used by both.
(4) IMPROVE: delisting treatment: integrate `dsedelist.parquet` so the last row return includes the delisting return (check how `equity_*.parquet` `ret` already handles it before building); add a "stop frequency / reason" table to the single-strategy report; engine performance for the matrix build.

## 6. Acceptance tests
- Equivalence: `ExitRule(max_hold=60)` reproduces `with_fixed_hold(uni, 60)` exit rows exactly on the synthetic panel and the real universe (assert equality of `exit_row_idx`); `steady_drift_plus` NAV unchanged when run through the new path (to the dollar).
- Stop counterexamples: path 100 -> 96 -> 94 stop 5%: exit day 3; wealth path 1.00 -> 1.035 -> 1.01 with activation 3% / trail 2%: peak 1.035, trigger level 1.035 x 0.98 = 1.0143, and 1.01 <= 1.0143, so the position exits at that close (activation is latched although the current return is below 3%; this is the P6 counterexample); path that never reaches +3% never activates.
- Gap-through: a day with -12% against a 5% stop exits at that close at -12%, not -5%.
- Delisting: stock with 7 rows left exits on its last row and the exit is marked `delist`.
- No-look-ahead: rewriting returns after exit day `k` does not change the exit row (hold the first k fixed, perturb k+1..H); trend gate: set `vwretd` on day0 and later to extreme values, gate for the event unchanged; gate NaN in the first 100 sessions -> zero weight; all-risk-off quarter -> no trades (P4 counterexample).
- Cap causality: rewrite the returns after a position's entry so that it stops out on day 3 instead of never; assert that the admission decisions (`notional` of every position entered up to that quarter's end) are IDENTICAL in both worlds (the cap uses scheduled, not actual, exits). This test fails on the current engine if `exit_row_idx` is simply replaced.
- Determinism and chunk invariance (chunk size 1 vs 20k identical).
- Two simultaneous +2% trades at 50% NAV each yield +2% NAV (P8 counterexample) in the engine summary.

## 7. Experiment plan
Baseline `steady_drift_plus` (12.07% / Sharpe 1.19 / DD -22.07%). Arms, all constants fixed in advance (no tuning): stop {none, 10%, 15%}; trailing (activation 5%, trail 3%); trend gate; each with and without the existing `risk_target_vol` throttle (the throttle already cuts exposure after drawdowns, so stops may be redundant: test the interaction explicitly). SEARCH day0_date <= 2005-12-31, HOLDOUT 2006-2013 (REF-period-split) (already seen for `steady_drift_plus`; state this). Metrics: return, Sharpe, max DD, Calmar, 2008 calendar-year return, win rate of exits by reason, share of stopped positions that would have recovered by day 60 (counterfactual from the positions table), turnover and cost drag. Placebo: apply the same stop to the shuffled-SUE book; a stop that "helps" equally the placebo is a generic risk effect, not PEAD-specific. Decision rule: adopt only if Sharpe and max DD both improve and 2008-13 Sharpe >= baseline's 0.79.

## 8. Expected outcome / hypotheses and risks
Hypothesis: stops reduce the 2008 drawdown a little but cost return in a drift whose payoff is slow; net Sharpe change near zero or negative; the throttle already does most of the job. Trailing stops with 5%/3% will mostly fire on noise for stocks with 3-4% daily vol (the book is sized to calm stocks, but ivol of small caps is high), raising turnover and costs. Trend gate was already looked at in `meta/` ("exposure choice, not skill"). Risks: close-based stops understate gap losses; bid/ask-average prices (`prc_is_bidask_avg`) can create fake stop triggers on illiquid names (mask: skip trigger on rows with that flag, then test sensitivity); data ends 2013.
