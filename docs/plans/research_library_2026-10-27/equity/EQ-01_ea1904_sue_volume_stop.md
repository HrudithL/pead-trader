# EQ-01: EA1904 classical rule (historical-sigma SUE x volume expansion, 20-session hold, 5% stop), clean-room, long-only
- Status: planned | Priority: P1 | Effort: M | Source(s): library `strategies/01_EA1904.md`, `audits/01_EA1904_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`; https://github.com/EA1904/pead-sp500-research (`backtest/run.py`, `backtest/portfolio_engine.py`)

## 1. Thesis & what we take from the source (and what we deliberately do NOT take)
Thesis: a surprise that is large relative to the firm's OWN history of surprises (not relative to analyst disagreement) and that arrives with unusual volume is a higher-conviction event; trade it for a short window with a protective stop.
Take: (a) `SUE_hist = (actual - pre-release consensus) / sigma_i`, sigma_i = std of the firm's prior surprises; (b) the volume-expansion gate; (c) the six parameter variants in `run.py` as a pre-registered grid (SUE 1.5 / 1.0 / 2.0, volume 1.5 / 0, hold 20 / 10, stop 5% / none); (d) a time exit of 10-20 sessions and a percent stop as the risk rule.
Do NOT take: the 77.73% / 81.88% numbers as calibration targets; the public `portfolio_engine.py` (unlagged, fully-invested, exit unbilled); the 25% vol target and ATR exits (not implemented in the public code; we have our own `risk_target_vol` throttle); the multiplex GAT (needs FactSet Revere edges, not here; see EQ-07 for the cheap proxy); the shorts (borrow not modelled; this is a long-only spec); the 2020 exclusion (our data ends 2013).

## 2. Evidence grade & known defects in the source
Library grade C/D, "NOT PUBLICLY REPRODUCIBLE" (`model.py` withheld). Defects (audit E1-E13): E2 signal[t] multiplied by return[t] with no shift (look-ahead if the signal uses day-t information); E3 close-only engine contradicts the claimed T+1 open; E4/E5 no ATR stop or vol target in the public engine and exits are never charged; E7 weight = 1/n_active, so one eligible name takes 100%; E8 the "buy and hold" benchmark is an equal-weight basket, not SPY; E10 annualisation uses row count and drops 2020; E11 DSR counts only six trials; E13 current-constituent fallback. The GAT adds +4.15 points over the classical rule and the trained model equals 10 random untrained graphs (Sharpe 2.259 vs 2.249, p=.30, AUC 0.497), so the interesting part is the classical rule, not the graph. Treat all author figures as unverified claims; the post-2020 S&P 500 sample is also a different regime from CRSP 1996-2013.

## 3. Exact rules
Event set: `equity_vol_panel` events with non-NaN `sue_hist`, `rvol_d` (EQ-02). Universe filter: `avg_dollar_volume_21d >= 2e6` (same floor as `steady_drift.MIN_DOLLAR_VOLUME`, kept for comparability).
Signal (fixed, from the library variants, no tuning): `sue_hist >= SUE_T` and `rvol_d >= VOL_T`. Baseline `SUE_T=1.5, VOL_T=1.5`. Long only.
Timing (default `entry_mode="day0_close"`): the decision is made at the day0 close (RVOL is a daily quantity and is only known at that close), the fill is the day0 close print, the first P&L day is day0+1. This is exactly how `run_equity_backtest` already works (P&L from `entry_row_idx+1`, verified in `pead/engines/equity.py`). Day0 is the first session in which the market can react (AMC releases are already shifted one session by the event builder; UNKNOWN time is treated as AMC). The gap and the day0 intraday leg are therefore never earned: this is a conservative stand-in for EA1904's "T+1 open". Optional `entry_mode="day1_open"`: order after the day0 close, filled at the day1 open (needs REF-dsf-reader + REF-entry-leg; first P&L row = close_day1/open_day1 - 1).
Exit: first of (i) `HOLD` sessions after entry (default 20), (ii) a close-based stop: close <= entry_close x (1 - STOP) (default 0.05); the exit fills at that day's close (no intrabar, no gap-through assumption beyond the close), (iii) the stock's last available row (delisting stays in P&L, same rule as `with_fixed_hold`). Implemented by `with_path_exits` (EQ-04).
Sizing: identical to `steady_drift_plus` so only the signal and exit differ: `Sizing(base_unit_fraction=0.003, max_gross_leverage=0.80, liquidity_cap_frac=0.05, return_basis="raw", cap_mode="chronological", exposure_cap="open_book", name_cap_frac=0.01)`, weights = `weights.causal_inverse_variance(events, mask)`, `long_only_cash=True`, `risk_target_vol=0.12`. Note: EA1904 uses 1/n_active equal weight; we do NOT (unbounded concentration, audit E7). An equal-weight capped arm (`weights = mask.astype(float)`) is an ablation only.
Costs: engine `COST_BPS` by liquidity quintile at entry and exit (`pead/engines/equity.py`); stress at 2x and 3x via an engine cost multiplier (see 5b). Borrow N/A (long only).
Stops fire on close-to-close data only; document that a real stop would gap through and fill worse.

## 4. Data requirements & availability in THIS repo
Available: IBES actual and consensus mean (84% coverage), CRSP daily `ret`, `prc`, `vol`, `cfacpr`. Needs EQ-02 (`sue_hist`, `rvol_d`). Missing: intraday volume (EA1904's "high-frequency" expansion); open prices only through REF-dsf-reader; point-in-time index membership (we use all CRSP names with a $2M floor, which includes small caps; the EA1904 universe is the S&P 500, so also run a size_quintile>=4 arm using `size_quintile` already in the events). Proxy: daily RVOL at day0 close.

## 6. Acceptance tests
- Synthetic stop test: price path 100 -> 96 -> 94 with 5% stop and entry at 100: exit at the close of the first day <= 95 (day 3), P&L equals -6%, cost charged at exit date; hold-20 position that never breaches exits at session 20; position of a stock delisting after 7 sessions exits at its last row.
- No-look-ahead: (a) rewrite all returns after day t and assert position decisions and exit row up to t unchanged (exit row is determined by closes <= exit day only); (b) truncation invariance of `sue_hist`/`rvol_d` (EQ-02); (c) shift test: set day0 return to +50% on every event and assert strategy P&L unchanged for `entry_mode="day0_close"` (the engine starts at day0+1) - fails loudly if anyone adds the day0 return.
- Library timing fixture: AMC release on Friday before a Monday holiday enters Tuesday's close, never Monday.
- Determinism: two runs give identical `final_nav` (float equality) and identical `positions` frame.
- Cap check: `max_gross_over_nav <= 0.80 + small` in summary; marked holdings test as in STEADY_DRIFT section 9.
- Equal-weight arm sanity: with one eligible name the weight is capped by `name_cap_frac`, never 100% (library audit E7 counterexample).

## 7. Experiment plan
Windows: SEARCH = day0_date <= 2005-12-31 (constants are the library's and fixed beforehand, so the search window selects nothing), HOLDOUT = 2006-01-01..2013-12-31 (REF-period-split, `pead/core/splits.py`; note: this holdout was already seen for `steady_drift_plus`, so it is a weak holdout, say so). Comparators from `results/leaderboard.md`: `steady_drift_plus` 12.07% / vol 9.97% / Sharpe 1.19 / DD -22.07%; `steady_drift` 10.59% / 1.16; `steady_drift_core` 11.99% / 0.93. Arms: (1) the 6 library variants; (2) same signal, 60-session hold and no stop (isolates the 20/5% exit from the signal); (3) `steady_drift_plus` with the stop added (EQ-04 overlap); (4) size_quintile >= 4 only; (5) day1_open entry once REF-entry-leg exists; (6) equal-weight capped. Placebo: shuffle `sue_hist` within quarter (3 seeds, as `meta/steady_placebo.py`), report real minus placebo (surprise skill; `steady_drift_plus` skill = +4.3 pts return, +3.9 pts alpha, +0.36 Sharpe per STEADY_DRIFT). Costs 1x/2x/3x. Judge vs `steady_drift_plus` on: Sharpe, alpha t (HAC, `alpha_stats`), 2008-13 Sharpe, skill-adjusted alpha, turnover (annual_turnover_x_capital), and a requirement that the improvement survives removing the best year. Do not call it better unless skill-adjusted alpha rises.

## 8. Expected outcome / hypotheses and risks
Honest expectation: no improvement over `steady_drift_plus`. In STEADY_DRIFT section 9 holds of 40 or 90 sessions lost ~2-3 points versus 60 and volume/confirmation filters among 35 variants "none beat the top-decile rule by more than noise". A 20-session hold with a 5% stop will cut turnover-adjusted return per position and raise cost drag (round-trip 5-25 bp each time). Hypothesis worth testing: `sue_hist` x RVOL selects a more event-specific subset, may help a lot in the 2008-13 era where rank-SUE alpha decayed to 4.1%. Risks: `sue_hist` rarely defined (84% consensus coverage, 8 priors); small number of events at SUE 2.0 x RVOL 1.5; stop on closes understates gap risk; the author numbers are for 2021-26 S&P 500 and cannot be compared; capacity not tested; data ends 2013; borrow/impact not modelled.
