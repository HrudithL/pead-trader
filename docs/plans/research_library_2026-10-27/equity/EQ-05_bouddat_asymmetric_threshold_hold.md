# EQ-05: Absolute-SUE thresholds with asymmetric holds (Bouddat: long SUE >= 0.635 for ~85 calendar days, short SUE < -3.78 for ~9 days), long-only first
- Status: planned | Priority: P1 (long leg, cheap) / P2 (short leg) | Effort: M | Source(s): library `strategies/04_ALADIN_BOUDDAT.md`, `audits/04_ALADIN_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`; https://github.com/aladinbouddat/PEAD-Strategy (`src/pead_strategy.py`, `output/backtest_result.txt`)

## 1. Thesis & what we take from the source (and what we deliberately do NOT take)
Thesis: winners drift for months, losers' drift is brief, so hold the long leg long and cover the short leg fast; and use ABSOLUTE SUE cut-offs (PIT by construction, no cross-sectional ranking) instead of deciles. This is the only source in the library built on the same data family as this repo (IBES analyst SUE `(actual - mean)/stdev`, CRSP, historical S&P members, 1994-2023), so it is the most directly comparable.
Take: (a) the signal scale (our `sue_analyst` is the same quantity); (b) the thresholds 0.635 / -3.78 as FIXED, pre-registered constants; (c) the holding-time asymmetry as an ablation axis (85/9, 85/85, 9/9, 20/20, long-only, as in library experiment F); (d) the cost components as stress inputs (flat 0.25% borrow APR, per-share commission), not as truth.
Do NOT take: entry at the event-date daily close regardless of release time (impossible for AMC); `adjust_to_weekday` calendar-day exits that can land on holidays; filling missing dispersion with the full-sample mean `surpstdev` (future information); the exit-price availability pre-filter (a forward-looking survivorship screen: stocks that cannot be exited are silently excluded); the spread formula `(ASK-BID)/(ASK+BID/2)`; fixed $5,649 order value (5.6% of initial capital, not scaling with NAV); ticker joins instead of PERMNO; the "Sharpe 0.0488" (a daily Sharpe, not annual; roughly 0.775 after x sqrt(252), the library's own conversion, not the author's).

## 2. Evidence grade & known defects in the source
Library grade B (code and saved result inspectable; WRDS data absent; not re-run). Reported: 1994-01-03 to 2023-12-31, 14.02% annualised vs SPY 9.68%, volatility 16.79% vs 18.68%, Jensen alpha 5.91%, 95,748 events from 1,337 historical S&P 500 members, costs $356,916 on $360.2M traded. Defects (audit A and section 3): same-day-close entry for after-close news; whole-sample fill of missing dispersion; future exit-price screening; spread formula underestimates cost; flat 0.25% APR borrow charged once on terminal notional; global mutable state; `spy.fillna(0)` risk; first-come-first-served capital; no corrected result exists and the library says not to invent one. Also note: with 14.02% vs 9.68% and volatility BELOW the index, part of the result may be a low-beta large-cap tilt; alpha must be re-estimated from daily returns with a proper factor model (the repo has `pead/core/alpha.py::alpha_stats`).

## 3. Exact rules
Signal (all from `sue_analyst_raw` in EQ-02, PIT: it uses only the pre-release consensus snapshot already in `ibes_clean_1995_2014.parquet`, with `n_lookahead_excluded` bookkeeping):
- `LONG` if `sue_source == "analyst"` and `sue_analyst_raw >= 0.635`.
- `SHORT` (P2 arm only) if `sue_analyst_raw < -3.78`.
- Verified in this repo's data: 48.6% of analyst-sourced events have SUE >= 0.635 and 6.6% have SUE < -3.78 (median SUE 0.5, p90 4.0, p99 12.5, min -1823). So the long rule is a very broad "any decent beat" screen, not a top-decile screen; expect many simultaneous candidates and a binding capital cap. Report the number of qualifying events per quarter.
- Missing dispersion: events with `consensus_stdev` missing/zero have NaN `sue_analyst` and are dropped (not filled). Optional arm `disp_fill="causal"`: fill with the firm's trailing mean of its own prior `consensus_stdev` (>= 2 priors, strictly earlier), else drop; never a sample-wide mean.
Entry: day0 close (framework default; AMC and UNKNOWN already shifted to the next session). Optional open-entry arm via EQ-03.
Exit (long): the last trading row on or before `day0_date + 85 calendar days` (never a non-trading date; if the stock has no row by then, its last available row = delisting stays in). Equivalent length ~60 sessions (85 x 5/7 = 60.7), the same horizon as `steady_drift*`; an arm `hold_mode="sessions", hold=60` is the control. Exit (short, P2): last row on or before `day0_date + 9 calendar days` (~6 sessions).
Sizing (long-only baseline = `steady_drift_plus` machinery so only the signal/hold differ): weights `W.causal_inverse_variance(events, mask)`, `Sizing(base_unit_fraction=0.003, max_gross_leverage=0.80, liquidity_cap_frac=0.05, return_basis="raw", cap_mode="chronological", exposure_cap="open_book", name_cap_frac=0.01)`, `long_only_cash=True`, `risk_target_vol=0.12`. Fidelity arm: equal weights (`weights = mask.astype(float)`), the source's fixed-notional-with-FCFS-cash behaviour approximated by `cap_mode="chronological"` (engine admits in announcement order, verified in `cap_open_book`).
Short leg (P2): signed negative weights, `return_basis="raw"`, flat borrow `short_borrow_apr` applied as `|notional| x apr x holding_calendar_days/360` at cover (the source formula) and stressed at 0.25% (source), 2%, 5%; short collateral/margin 50% reduces investable cash (the source's `margin=0.5`); no cash rebate. Because the repo found the short leg unprofitable once collateral is counted ("short leg nets -4.4 pts", STEADY_DRIFT), the short arm is a measurement of whether an EXTREME tail (SUE < -3.78) plus a 9-day cover differs, not an expectation.
Costs: engine `COST_BPS` by liquidity quintile at both ends; commissions of the source ($0.0035/share) are negligible next to the bps schedule and not modelled separately.

## 4. Data requirements & availability in THIS repo
Available: `sue_analyst` (and `consensus_*`) in `data/events/equity_events_1996_2013.parquet`; CRSP daily panel; PERMNO keys (no ticker joins); `ret_fwd_{1,5,10,20,40,60}d(_mktadj)` precomputed in the same event table, which allows a fast portfolio-free screen. Missing: historical S&P 500 membership (the source's universe) - we use all CRSP names with a $2M floor, so also run `size_quintile == 5` (the proxy for large caps) and compare; `dsedelist.parquet` delisting returns not integrated; stock borrow data (none; flat APR only); bid/ask spread series (CRSP `bid/ask` exist in raw `dsf_*.parquet` but not in the panel). Coverage: 1996-2013, source 1994-2023; no overlap after 2013.

## 6. Acceptance tests
- Synthetic: event on 2000-01-03 (Mon) with 85 cal days -> exit row is the last trading row <= 2000-03-28 (never a holiday or weekend), asserted against an explicit holiday list; stock with data ending before that exits at its last row.
- Threshold edge cases: SUE exactly 0.635 included; exactly -3.78 excluded from short (strict `<`); NaN excluded; `sue_source == "seasonal"` excluded.
- No-look-ahead: (a) appending future EPS rows leaves earlier signals unchanged (EQ-02 truncation test); (b) dispersion fill uses only strictly earlier observations (rewrite later `consensus_stdev` and assert unchanged); (c) day0 P&L never earned (set day0 return +50%, P&L unchanged); (d) AMC fixture: 16:10 release on Monday trades Tuesday's close.
- Survivorship: a position whose stock is delisted before the planned exit is KEPT (source defect "future exit price filter" counterexample).
- Borrow: short with notional 1,000, 9 calendar days, 0.25% APR -> borrow cost = 1000 x 0.0025 x 9/360 = $0.0625 exactly (the source formula), stress 5% scales linearly.
- Short ROI: short entered at 100 covered at 80 earns +20% of notional (not +25%).
- Determinism; cap check (`max_gross_over_nav <= 0.80 + eps`); long-only books show `n_short == 0`.

## 7. Experiment plan
Step 0 (portfolio-free, minutes): mean market-adjusted `ret_fwd_h` for SUE buckets (< -3.78, -3.78..-1.5, -1.5..0, 0..0.635, 0.635..1.5, 1.5..4, >= 4) at h = 5/10/20/40/60, clustered by quarter, by era and by `size_quintile`; this tells whether a 0.635 cut is on a flat part of the curve (if drift at 0.635..1.5 is ~0, the broad screen is just universe exposure). Step 1: `bouddat_long` vs `bouddat_long_60s` vs `steady_drift_plus`; arms: threshold {0.635 (source), the SUE value matching the 90th percentile of the same pool (diagnostic only)}, size_quintile 5 only, weight mode equal vs inverse variance, 85 vs 60 sessions-equivalent. Step 2 (P2): asymmetric L/S with 85/9, 85/85, 9/9, 20/20, long-only; borrow 0.25% / 2% / 5%. Splits: SEARCH day0_date <= 2005-12-31, HOLDOUT 2006-2013 (REF-period-split) (already seen for `steady_drift_plus`, say so). Constants (0.635, -3.78, 85, 9) are the source's and are NOT re-optimised; any new threshold is exploratory and labelled in-sample. Placebo: shuffle SUE within quarter among qualifying-universe events, same machine. Comparators: `steady_drift_plus` 12.07% / Sharpe 1.19 / DD -22.07%; `steady_drift` 10.59%; market-neutral best `calm_rank_tilt` 6.01% / Sharpe 1.47 (levered 1.5-2.7x gross). Judge on skill-adjusted alpha (real minus placebo), 2008-13 Sharpe, and capital utilisation.

## 8. Expected outcome / hypotheses and risks
H1: the long leg at 0.635 will have lower Sharpe than the top-decile book because ~half of all events qualify, diluting surprise signal (STEADY_DRIFT: top two deciles instead of one cost 1.5-2 pts and 0.2 Sharpe). H2: the 85 calendar-day hold will match 60 sessions closely (same horizon), which independently supports the repo's 60-session constant. H3: the short leg at SUE < -3.78 with a 9-day cover will net near zero after borrow stress (repo: shorts do not pay once collateral and borrow are counted). Risks: source results are S&P 500 large caps over 1994-2023 with a broad index benchmark; our universe is mostly small caps pre-2013; no borrow data; selection by FCFS makes early-season announcers over-weighted; the 0.635 and -3.78 numbers may be tuned on the source's own sample (unknown) so treat them as one pre-registered point, not an optimum; data ends 2013; capacity not examined.
