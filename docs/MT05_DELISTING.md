# MT-05: delisting returns and event survivorship

`pead/core/delisting.py` compounds the CRSP terminal return onto each stock's last panel row ((1+ret)(1+dlret)-1; a missing dlret on a performance delisting, codes 4xx-5xx, is imputed at -30% NYSE/AMEX and -55% Nasdaq, the Shumway conventions, which are parameters). 3,970 delistings in `dsedelist`, 3,809 with a recorded dlret, 149 imputed. Variants `steady_drift_plus_dlret`, `_dlret_only`, `_dlret_half`.

**Result: no change.** All three variants reproduce `steady_drift_plus` to the cent (final NAV 91,947,400; 12.07% / Sharpe 1.19). The reason is upstream of the engine: the event table keeps only events with a full holding window (minimum `holding_days` is 31; 417 of 172,923 events have fewer than 60 sessions left, 1 of them in the top SUE decile), so a stock that delists soon after its announcement is not an event at all. Of the 21,091 top-decile analyst events in `decile_events_secid_all`, 205 have fewer than 60 forward sessions and 101 fewer than 31; their mean 20-session forward return (+2.6%) is not worse than the decile's (+2.1%). So the delisting correction is correct and in place (3 tests) but cannot matter until events are retained regardless of outcome (MT-02 phase 3: keep every announcement in the ranking pool and mark short-lived ones untradable). Given the counts, the expected effect of that change on `steady_drift_plus` is small (about 0.5% of top-decile events), but it has not been measured with outcomes attached.
